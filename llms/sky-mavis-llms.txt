# Sky Mavis (Ronin)

> Sky Mavis is the studio behind Axie Infinity and Ronin, an EVM blockchain built for games with player-owned economies. Its developer platform exposes API-gateway products for building on Ronin — the Skynet Web3 data API, a Ronin JSON-RPC endpoint, Axie Infinity game-data APIs, Ronin Market/Store partner APIs, and Ronin Waypoint (an OAuth2/OIDC account service and keyless wallet). Gateway products authenticate with an X-API-KEY from the Ronin Developer Console; public JSON-RPC and the Waypoint OIDC surface are openly reachable.

## APIs
- [Ronin Skynet Web3 API](https://docs.skymavis.com/): REST Web3 data over Ronin — accounts, NFTs, tokens, blocks, contracts, transactions. Base: https://api-gateway.skymavis.com/skynet (X-API-KEY).
- [Ronin JSON-RPC API](https://docs.skymavis.com/): Ethereum-compatible JSON-RPC. Public mainnet base: https://api.roninchain.com/rpc (chain 2020).
- [Ronin Waypoint Account Service](https://docs.skymavis.com/mavis/ronin-waypoint/overview): OAuth2/OIDC account + keyless wallet. Issuer: https://athena.skymavis.com/.
- [Axie Infinity Origins API](https://docs.skymavis.com/): Game data — items, leaderboards, battles, seasons (X-API-KEY).
- [Ronin Market / Store Partner APIs](https://docs.skymavis.com/): NFT marketplace and in-game store integration (X-API-KEY).
